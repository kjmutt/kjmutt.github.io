The festive highlights

Three countries, three Christmas market stops

The Christmas markets were a highlight of our family holiday. We visited markets in Zurich, Switzerland, Nuremberg, Germany, and Salzburg, Austria—three different city settings connected by one festive trip.

A Christmas journey that began before sunrise

Our Christmas journey began at Bristol Airport before sunrise. Zurich was our first stop, and ahead of us lay a train across the border, Christmas with family in Germany and a visit to Austria. By the time we returned on New Year’s Day, we had travelled through three countries in thirteen days.

The trip had two rhythms: our first two nights in Switzerland, followed by a longer stay with family in Landshut. Along the way, Christmas markets in Zurich, Nuremberg and Salzburg gave us three very different glimpses of the festive season.

01 / Switzerland · 20–22 December 2025

An early start for Zurich

On 20 December, our family boarded the early easyJet flight from Bristol to Zurich. It was booked to leave at 06:35 and arrive at 09:15 local time, giving us the rest of the day to begin exploring Switzerland.

The trip’s dated location records place us in Zürich on 20 December and Hergiswil, Switzerland, on 21 December. Zurich remained our opening base until 22 December, when we continued to Germany from Zürich Hauptbahnhof, the city’s main railway station. It gave the holiday a clear two-part beginning: arriving by air, then leaving the city by rail.

A tiny recording from the evening of 21 December keeps another moment from Zürich: illuminated buildings and a passing train seen through the window. Watch the Zürich night scene →

Zurich: Christmas markets and the Singing Christmas Tree

In Zurich, we found ourselves among Christmas market stalls and festive decorations. One scene I filmed on 20 December stayed with me: a choir arranged on a tree-shaped stage. It matches the Singing Christmas Tree at Werdmühleplatz, just off Bahnhofstrasse.

Zurich has several distinct festive markets, including those in the Old Town and inside Zürich HB. Our own photograph below records our Christmas market visit in the city, with a decorated tree and festive stalls.

02 / Switzerland to Germany · 22 December

From Zürich HB to Munich, without changing trains

Two days later, it was time to trade the flight for a train. On 22 December, we boarded Deutsche Bahn EC 193 at Zürich HB, booked to leave at 13:32 and arrive in Munich at 17:05. It was a direct journey of 3 hours 33 minutes—one train carrying us from Switzerland into the German part of our holiday.

For our family, the direct connection kept this part of the route straightforward: one train between the Swiss and German city stops. Our booking covered two adults and one child on Super Sparpreis Europa tickets; no seats were reserved.

Germany · A Christmas market highlight

Nuremberg’s famous Christkindlesmarkt

On 23 December, we visited the famous Christmas market in Nuremberg (Nürnberg). Our photograph captures the illuminated Christkindlesmarkt sign above a busy street—a moment that brings us straight back to that evening. In the old town’s Hauptmarkt, the Christkindlesmarkt is known for wooden stalls, red-and-white roofs, Christmas decorations and gingerbread.

It was one of the key festive stops alongside Zurich and Salzburg. Our photograph confirms the visit on 23 December 2025, during the German leg of our holiday.

Market setting: Hauptmarkt, Nuremberg, Germany. The photograph above is from our own visit on 23 December 2025.

03 / Germany · Christmas 2025

A family base in Landshut

After the Zurich–Munich journey, Landshut became our base for the German part of the holiday. Staying with family shaped this section of the trip, with Christmas itself part of a longer stay rather than a brief city break.

Our location records also place us in Schwangau on Christmas Eve, 24 December, and Landshut on 31 December. These add two dated stops to the German part of the holiday. The records do not by themselves establish admission to a particular castle or attraction.

Munich was the arrival point for the train and the departure point for our flight home. Landshut provided the family connection between those two journeys. We stayed in this part of Germany from 22 December until our return on 1 January.

Christmas Eve · 24 December 2025

Snow in Schwangau

Christmas Eve brought us to Schwangau in Bavaria, Germany. A horse-drawn carriage came towards us along a snow-covered woodland road. Later that afternoon, we walked beneath white branches as snow fell around the path. After the Christmas market streets of Nuremberg, this was a quieter winter scene.

Christmas Day · 25 December 2025

Mountain views and Christmas lights in Innsbruck

On Christmas morning, a short recording places us on the motorway near Irschenberg, Bavaria, with snow-dusted trees beside the road. By the afternoon, our holiday had taken us into Austria, to Innsbruck.

From Hungerburg, we looked down across Innsbruck and the River Inn towards the mountain skyline. The low afternoon sun shone near the peaks. Later, back at street level, a red tram passed beneath sparkling Christmas decorations: a small everyday journey framed by the festive city.

04 / Austria · Late December 2025

Adding Salzburg to the holiday

After Christmas, our journey took us across another border to Salzburg, Austria, on 29 December 2025. Switzerland had given us the first market scenes; Germany had brought family time and Nuremberg. Salzburg added another festive city to the story.

Salzburg: Christmas stalls in the old town

We visited Salzburg’s Christmas market during our 29 December stop. The Salzburger Christkindlmarkt is centred on Domplatz and Residenzplatz beside Salzburg Cathedral. Its 2025 season continued through 1 January 2026, so this was a market visit after Christmas Day.

My Salzburg footage includes a miniature railway and model village in a festive outdoor display. It adds a small moving detail to the wider market story.

05 / Germany to the UK · 1 January 2026

Home on New Year’s Day

We returned from Munich Terminal 1 to Bristol on easyJet flight EZY2944 on Thursday 1 January 2026. The booked departure was 20:25, with arrival at 21:35 local time.

Our New Year’s Day flight from Munich to Bristol closed a journey that had started in Zurich. Looking back, I remember it through the places and moments that connected the route: the choir in Zurich, the train to Munich, family time in Landshut, Nuremberg’s illuminated market and our Salzburg visit.
