Our week in numbers

Our week in Greece took us across the mainland and out to the islands. We covered roughly 490 miles by ferry, 425 miles by road and 12 miles on foot—plenty of changing views along the way.

Before the Christmas markets, there was Greece

Months before our winter journey through Switzerland, Germany and Austria, we travelled to Greece for a week in April. This holiday had a different shape: three nights in Athens, two on Mykonos, one on Santorini and one final night back in the capital before flying home.

Athens was our gateway, while the island hotels gave the middle of the week its rhythm. There was also a day reserved for the mainland landscape of Meteora.

01 / Athens · 12–15 April

An evening arrival in Athens

We began at Gatwick’s North Terminal, with our car booked into Long Stay North from 14:00 on 12 April until 01:00 on 20 April. That parking window gave us time on either side of the flights, including the late return to the UK on 19 April.

Our trip began on Saturday 12 April 2025 with an easyJet flight from London Gatwick to Athens. Our flight was U26549, departing at 16:10 and arriving at 21:50, both local times. The late arrival made Athens our first overnight stop.

For the opening three nights, our hotel was Airotel Alexandros Hotel on Timoleontos Vassou Street. We stayed here from 12 to 15 April before continuing to the islands.

Athens stay · 12–15 April · 3 nightsAirotel Alexandros Hotel, 8 Timoleontos Vassous Street, Athens, Attiki, 11521, Greece.

We visited the Acropolis Museum on Dionysiou Areopagitou and its café, and spent time among the ancient landmarks on the Acropolis. From high above the city, the rooftops stretched towards the surrounding hills.

02 / Mainland Greece · 14 April

A day set aside for Meteora

While based in Athens, we booked a one-day Meteora tour with a local guide for Monday 14 April 2025, through Meteora Thrones. Our family of three had an 08:00 bus departure from outside Athens' Larissa railway station on Theodore Diligianni Street. We met outside the station for the bus journey north.

Meteora is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for monasteries perched on towering rock formations. Its dramatic rock formations brought a completely different landscape into our week between Athens and the islands.

03 / Mykonos · 15–17 April

From the capital to an island base

Tuesday 15 April marked the change from our Athens hotel to Mykonos. We stayed at Croco Mykonos in Tourlos, for two nights, checking out on 17 April. Our room had a side sea view.

Leaving Athens behind, we continued from Piraeus towards Mykonos, trading the mainland streets for the next island chapter of our holiday.

Mykonos stay · 15–17 April · 2 nightsCroco Mykonos, Μύκονος, Tourlos, 84600, Greece. Superior Double Room with Side Sea View.

Tourlos connected our Mykonos stay with the ferry terminal. With two nights here, we had a pause between the mainland journey and our shorter stay on Santorini.

04 / Santorini · 17–18 April

One night in a cave house

Our next stop was Santorini, with a stay at Drops Cave Houses in Vóthon for Thursday 17 April to Friday 18 April. A cave studio with a private plunge pool gave this one-night stay a different character from the hotels on either side of it.

Our Santorini stop brought us to Vóthon and Fira. Lucky’s Souvlakis, the KTEL bus station and Athinios port were some of the smaller stops between our cave-house stay and the next sea crossing.

Santorini stay · 17–18 April · 1 nightDrops Cave Houses, Drops Collection 1, Vóthon, 84700, Greece. Cave Studio with Private Plunge Pool.

05 / Santorini to Athens to the UK · 18–19 April

A long crossing, then one last night

The return towards Athens was booked on Blue Star Delos on Friday 18 April: Santorini at 15:30 to Piraeus at 23:20. That scheduled crossing of seven hours and fifty minutes put the sea journey at the centre of our final full day in Greece.

Our last hotel was Titania Hotel in Athens, checking in on 18 April and out on 19 April. It completed the loop: back in the city where the holiday had begun, ready for the flight home.

Final Athens stay · 18–19 April · 1 nightTitania Hotel, 52 Panepistimiou Avenue, Athens.

On Saturday 19 April 2025, our Wizz Air UK W95746 flight left Athens at 20:25 and arrived at London Gatwick at 22:25 local time. The trip had taken us from the capital to two islands and back in seven nights.

Along the way

The smaller stops between the main destinations

In Piraeus, the railway station, Gelasakis Travel, Lili Drogerie and Freddopolitan formed part of the everyday side of the journey: transport, a little shopping and a coffee stop between our main destinations.
