These are Timeline estimates rather than measured journey totals; the driving category does not establish that we personally drove and may include other road transport.

Before the Christmas markets, there was Greece

Months before our winter journey through Switzerland, Germany and Austria, we travelled to Greece for a week in April. This holiday had a different shape: three nights in Athens, two on Mykonos, one on Santorini and one final night back in the capital before flying home.

Looking back through the bookings brings the sequence into focus. Athens was our gateway, while the island hotels gave the middle of the week its rhythm. There was also a day reserved for the mainland landscape of Meteora.

01 / Athens · 12–15 April

An evening arrival in Athens

Our trip began on Saturday 12 April 2025 with an easyJet flight from London Gatwick to Athens. The confirmation lists flight U26549, departing at 16:10 and arriving at 21:50, both local times. The late arrival made Athens our first overnight stop.

For the opening three nights, our hotel was Airotel Alexandros Hotel on Timoleontos Vassou Street. The hotel confirmation runs from 12 to 15 April. An earlier Ace Hotel reservation had been cancelled, so Airotel is the stay that belongs in this account.

Athens stay · 12–15 April · 3 nightsAirotel Alexandros Hotel, 8 Timoleontos Vassou Street, Athens.

Athens gave us a base before the journey continued towards the islands. Your Google Maps Timeline adds a named cultural stop: the Acropolis Museum on Dionysiou Areopagitou, together with its café and restaurant. This gives our Athens chapter a landmark beyond the hotel and airport. The museum listing does not, by itself, establish a separate visit to the Acropolis hill or the Parthenon.

02 / Mainland Greece · 14 April

A day set aside for Meteora

While based in Athens, we booked a one-day Meteora tour with a local guide for Monday 14 April 2025, through Meteora Thrones. The booking was for three people, with an 08:00 bus departure from outside Athens' Larissa railway station on Theodore Diligianni Street. Although the meeting point was a railway station, the confirmation specifies a bus.

Meteora is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for monasteries perched on towering rock formations. It is the clearest named landmark in the Greece booking trail, bringing a mainland excursion into a holiday otherwise structured around Athens and the islands.

The dated tour confirmation establishes the booked excursion. The Timeline also lists Travel Stop Meteora, a café stop. It supports a stop in the Meteora travel area, while the particular monasteries entered remain unverified.

03 / Mykonos · 15–17 April

From the capital to an island base

Tuesday 15 April marked the change from our Athens hotel to Mykonos. Our next reservation was at Croco Mykonos in Tourlos, for two nights, checking out on 17 April. The room was booked as a Superior Double Room with a side sea view.

The initial ferry confirmation was for Piraeus to Mykonos at 07:30 on Blue Star Paros. The emails also show requests to change that arrangement. Messages from Croco Mykonos on the arrival morning confirm that a transfer was being arranged, including a revised 13:20 time, but they do not conclusively identify the final ship. That is why the original ferry booking is recorded as an initial plan rather than a verified completed sailing.

Mykonos stay · 15–17 April · 2 nightsCroco Mykonos, Tourlos. Superior Double Room with Side Sea View.

The Timeline records both Croco Mykonos and the Mykonos ferry terminal in Tourlos, supporting this part of our island route. With two nights here, Mykonos was more than a connection between boats: it was our first island base before the shorter Santorini stay that followed.

04 / Santorini · 17–18 April

One night in a cave house

Our next stop was Santorini, with a reservation at Drops Cave Houses in Vóthon for Thursday 17 April to Friday 18 April. A cave studio with a private plunge pool gave this one-night stay a different character from the hotels on either side of it.

The hotel's message on 16 April asked for our arrival details so they could arrange our welcome. Together with the dated reservation and the return ferry booking, it places Santorini between Mykonos and our final Athens night. The exact Mykonos–Santorini service has not yet been verified.

Santorini stay · 17–18 April · 1 nightDrops Cave Houses, Vóthon. Cave Studio with Private Plunge Pool.

The Timeline adds Fira, with entries for Lucky’s Souvlakis on Dekigala and KTEL Santorini, as well as Thira Santorini Port at Órmos Athiniós. These connect our Vóthon stay with the island’s town and transport stops. A bus-company listing does not identify the exact service we rode. Oia, individual blue-domed churches and a particular sunset viewpoint remain unverified; the city-card cover photographs are illustrative Google images rather than evidence of our visits.

05 / Santorini to Athens to the UK · 18–19 April

A long crossing, then one last night

The return towards Athens was booked on Blue Star Delos on Friday 18 April: Santorini at 15:30 to Piraeus at 23:20. That scheduled crossing of seven hours and fifty minutes put the sea journey at the centre of our final full day in Greece.

Our last hotel reservation was Titania Hotel in Athens, checking in on 18 April and out on 19 April. It completed the loop: back in the city where the holiday had begun, ready for the flight home.

Final Athens stay · 18–19 April · 1 nightTitania Hotel, 52 Panepistimiou Avenue, Athens.

On Saturday 19 April 2025, our booked Wizz Air UK W95746 flight left Athens at 20:25 and arrived at London Gatwick at 22:25 local time. The trip had taken us from the capital to two islands and back in seven nights.
